02 · Colour

Brand palette

Four colours form the entire brand palette. Primary Blue is the dominant colour — it anchors every touchpoint. Surface White provides breathing room. Ink is reserved for body text only. Mid Blue appears in secondary typography and UI accents.

03 · Typography

Type system

Three typefaces. Each has a specific role — do not substitute or mix roles. All three are available as free Google Fonts.

Aa
Libre Baskerville
Headings · wordmark
A confident, institutional serif. Used for all headings, the wordmark, and document titles.
Aa
IBM Plex Sans
Body copy · navigation
Clean and legible at all sizes. Use weights 300–500 only. No bold in body copy.
Aa
IBM Plex Mono
Labels · data · codes
Reserved for labels, metadata, reference codes, and any data output.

05 · Rules

Do and don't

Do
Use the logo on Primary Blue, Surface White, or plain white only.
Don't
Place the logo on colourful, patterned, or photographic backgrounds.
Do
Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "S" in SALIENT on all sides.
Don't
Stretch, rotate, recolour, or rearrange any element of the logo.
Do
Use the mark alone (without wordmark) only at sizes below 40px or in favicon contexts.
Don't
Use gradients, drop shadows, or decorative effects on or around the logo.
Do
Always use the reversed (white) variant when placing the logo on Primary Blue backgrounds.
Don't
Use any typeface other than Libre Baskerville in the wordmark, even for internal documents.
